Discord

Posts to a channel through a webhook.

Getting a webhook URL
  1. In your Discord server, right-click the channel and choose Edit Channel.
  2. Open Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook.
  3. Copy the webhook URL and paste it above.

Email

Sends through any SMTP server.

Turn this off for SSL/TLS on port 465. Gmail needs an app password, not your regular password.
Setting up a Gmail app password
  1. Go to Google Account → Security.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification if it isn't on already.
  3. Search for "App Passwords" and create one for "Mail".
  4. Use that 16-character password above, not your Gmail password.

Telegram

Messages a chat or group through your own bot.

Setting up a Telegram bot
  1. In Telegram, search for @BotFather and send /newbot.
  2. Follow the prompts and paste the bot token above.
  3. Add the bot to your group, or start a direct message with it.
  4. To find the chat ID, send the bot a message, then open https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/getUpdates.
  5. The number in "chat":{"id": ...} is your chat ID. Group chats use a negative number (e.g. -1001234567890).
